Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 35 Collective LLC, doing business as Thomas Productions (“THOMAS”, “we”, “us”), collects, uses, and protects information when you visit thomasproductionsco.com or engage our services. By using our site or services, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you contact us, request a quote, book a call, or engage our services, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, company, property details, billing information, and any information you choose to share with us.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our site, we may automatically collect your IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, referring URLs, and similar analytics data through cookies and comparable technologies.

2. Use of Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide quotes
  • Schedule, produce, edit, and deliver photo, video, drone, and social media work
  • Process payments and manage accounts
  • Communicate about projects, updates, and relevant offers
  • Improve our site and services
  • Comply with legal obligations

Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal information on the basis of your consent, the performance of a contract with you, our legitimate business interests, and compliance with applicable law.

3. Disclosure of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate (e.g., scheduling, payment processing, cloud storage, and production partners), with your property or organization where you have engaged us on their behalf, and where required by law or to protect our rights.

4. File Uploads and Media Content

Photos, videos, and other media we capture or that you provide are stored securely and used to deliver and promote the work you engaged us for. We will not publicly publish identifiable media of your property in our portfolio or marketing without a reasonable basis to believe such use is permitted. Contact us at any time to request that specific media not be used publicly.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site, remember preferences, and understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect site functionality.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention of Information

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide our services, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

8. Florida and United States Privacy Rights

Depending on where you reside, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond consistent with applicable law.

9. Third-Party Websites

Our site may link to third-party websites and services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties, and we encourage you to review their policies.

10. Children’s Information

Our services are directed to businesses and are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above reflects the most recent revision, and continued use of our site or services constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Advertising and Analytics

We may use analytics and advertising tools (such as social and search platform tags) to measure performance and reach relevant audiences. These tools may set their own cookies subject to their respective privacy policies.

13. “Do Not Track” Signals

Our site does not currently respond differently to browser “Do Not Track” signals.
