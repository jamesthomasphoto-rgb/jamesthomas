## Style
- Clean sans-serif titles, white or off-white, lower-third or centered. Minimal.
- Slow, steady camera moves on stills. Nothing faster than a slow drift.
- Music: modern, cinematic, no vocals. Build through section 3, settle for 4–5, resolve at 6.
- Colour: match the renders (dusk light, warm wood, bronze/black). Don't over-grade the aerials; keep them natural.
- No watermark, no placeholder text. If a render has placeholder signage, crop or avoid it.
