## 1. What this is (read first)
A marketing video for an apartment tower in Tampa that **has not been built yet**. It's being shown to **investors**. It has to answer one question: **"Why this building, in this location?"**

Three things must come through, in this priority:
1. **The building:** what it will look like, inside and out (from the renders).
2. **The location:** a busy, popular neighborhood with lots to do (from the real drone footage).
3. **The surroundings:** what's nearby and how close it is (from the 3D map with callouts).

Tone: polished, calm, confident, high-end commercial real estate. Think brokerage or developer launch film. **Not** a social reel.

## 2. Hard rules (any of these = recut)
- **The building must look exactly like the renders.** Don't warp, AI-extend, recolour or "improve" the architecture. Bronze/black frame, warm wood soffits, slatted podium screen, palms, dusk light: all stay exactly as drawn.
- **No placeholder text on screen.** Some renders and concept images show "SIGNAGE" or "THE LOFT" on the building. Crop it out, mask it, or don't use that frame.
- **No invented facts.** Every number on screen comes from Section 7. If something's missing, use a clearly marked placeholder (`[UNITS]`), never a guess.
- **No people added**, no stock footage, no logos other than the ones we send you.
- **No fast or flashy transitions:** no whip pans, glitches, light leaks or spinning. Use cuts and short cross-dissolves (8–12 frames) only.
- **Camera moves on stills are slow:** 3–8% scale change over the shot. Never faster than a slow drift.
- **All titles must be live, editable text layers** (we swap the real name in at the end). Don't burn them into the picture.
- Don't contact anyone else about this project. Questions go to us only.

## 4. Drone footage: how to choose
- Horizontal, 16:9. Use **only** stable, in-focus, well-exposed clips.
- **Favour activity:** cars, people walking, open shops. It should feel busy and popular.
- Skip anything cloudy or grey, shaky, or full of propellers, glare or lens flare.
- Speed: real time, or slowed to 50–60% if it was shot at 50/60 fps. No speed ramps.
- If a clip shows the empty lot or parking area where the tower will go, keep it. We need it for "building in place".

---

## 5. Look, type and sound
- **Colour:** match the renders (warm dusk, rich but natural). Grade the drone footage lightly so it sits next to the renders, without over-saturating. No heavy LUTs.
- **Type:** one clean modern sans-serif (Montserrat, Inter, Helvetica Neue or similar). White, with a soft shadow if needed. All-caps for titles, sentence case for lower-thirds. Bottom-left lower-thirds, inside title-safe.
- **Music:** licensed (Artlist / Musicbed / Epidemic). Modern, cinematic, no vocals, ~90–110 BPM. Build through sections 1–2, lift at the map, peak at the amenities, resolve on the close. Send us the track name.
- **Audio:** music only. No drone audio, no VO. Master to around -16 LUFS, no clipping. Fade in at the start and out at the end.
