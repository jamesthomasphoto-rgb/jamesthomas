## I. Overview — Build the business. Build yourself.

This is the exact process I've used to build my own companies and to train the people who now run them. It isn't theory. It's what I did, written down in order, so you can do it too.

> My process is simple: you build the business, and you build yourself. Not two projects. One. — James Thomas

A finished business has a definition. You can leave it, sell it, or run it from your phone on the other side of the world — and it holds. Anything less is a job you own. Everything in these ninety days is built toward that finish line: time freedom, location freedom and financial freedom, designed in from day one instead of hoped for at the end.

## II. The Five Stages

Everyone runs all ninety days, in order. Your stage tells you where to push hardest. Most people are one stage behind where they think they are. That's normal, and it's the fastest way through.

1. **Stage 0 — Starting from zero.** No business yet. You want an online business and the freedom that comes with it. *Push hardest: Course 02*
2. **Stage 1 — Doing it all.** Freelancer or solo operator. You find the work, do the work, chase the invoice. Feast or famine. *Push hardest: Courses 03–05*
3. **Stage 2 — It runs through you.** Real revenue, real clients — and every one of them needs you. Around $10–30K a month. *Push hardest: Courses 06–09*
4. **Stage 3 — A team, not a machine.** People on payroll, but you're still the bottleneck. Around $30–100K a month. *Push hardest: Courses 09–12*
5. **Stage 4 — Owner, not operator.** It runs without you. Now it's about scale, deals, and the life it pays for. *Push hardest: Courses 13–14*

## III. The 90-Day Program — Three Moves

The program is ninety days: one move a month, one course a week, one live call a week. It's the same path I walked my brother through — from someone who just wanted to have a company, to running one fully remote at six figures a month.

1. **Money In** (Days 1–30 · Weeks 1–4) — An offer people understand, a steady flow of leads, and you selling it with confidence. Cash coming in every week.
2. **The Machine** (Days 31–60 · Weeks 5–8) — Someone else delivers. Systems, SOPs, a CRM, your first hires. Clients stay, and the work moves without you touching it.
3. **Step Out** (Days 61–90 · Weeks 9–12) — The numbers run on a scoreboard, the team runs the day, and you run the company from your phone — anywhere.

**Rich · Fit · Sick runs underneath all ninety days. Rich — the work, the learning, a rich life. Fit — train every day. Sick — mindset, reading, style, the standard you hold. The business and the person get built at the same time.**

## VIII. How It Runs

**The weekly rhythm**

- **Monday** — Post your target for the week and the one number you're moving.
- **The live call** — Thirty minutes of teaching, thirty minutes of your questions. Recorded into the Classroom.
- **Friday** — Post your wins and your misses. Both count.

**The rules of the room**

1. **Show your work.** Wins, misses, work in progress. This room runs on real reps.
2. **Ask specific questions.** “How do I get clients?” gets less than “Here's my offer, my market, and what's broken.”
3. **What's shared here stays here.** Members' numbers and businesses are private.
4. **No pitching.** Help people. If you've helped someone, a DM is fine.
5. **Do the assignments.** Every course ends with one. That's where the results are.
